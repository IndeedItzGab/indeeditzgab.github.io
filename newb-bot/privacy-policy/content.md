# Privacy Policy
Last Modified: 28th of September 2026.

## Introduction
Your privacy is important to us. It is Newb Bot's policy to respect your privacy and comply with applicable laws and regulations regarding the collection and use of information.

Account Information refers to information that can be used to identify your Discord account. This includes information associated with your Discord account, such as your user ID, display username, display avatar, and other relevant account details provided by Discord.

We do not collect personal information about you outside of information associated with your Discord account, such as your real name, physical address, date of birth, device information, email address, or phone number, unless otherwise stated in this Privacy Policy.

## Information We Collect
**Account Information**: Newb Bot may collect information associated with your Discord account, such as your user ID, display username, display avatar, and other relevant account details.

**Usage Data**: Newb Bot may store data generated through your use of the bot and its features. This data is used to operate, maintain, improve, and develop our services, including automation and other features within the community server.

## Third-Party Services
The bot may use third-party services to provide certain features or functionality. These services may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms of service.

## Data Retention
Newb Bot will retain information only for as long as necessary to provide its features, maintain the bot, comply with applicable requirements, or fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

When information is no longer necessary, it may be deleted from our databases and systems.

## Data Deletion
If you would like information associated with your Discord account to be deleted, please contact the bot developer using the email address provided below. In your request, include your Discord user ID and a description of the data you would like to have deleted.
